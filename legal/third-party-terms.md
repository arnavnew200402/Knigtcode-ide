KnightCode is free software you run on your own machine. We operate no service
you could misuse, so there is no acceptable-use policy of ours to state.

Two other parties' policies do reach you through KnightCode, and this page
exists to point at them rather than to restate them:

- **Your AI provider.** Whichever account you sign in to — Anthropic, OpenAI,
  GitHub Copilot, or the one your API key belongs to — its acceptable-use
  policy governs what you may ask a model to do. KnightCode sends your prompts
  to them directly and imposes nothing of its own.
- **The Zed extension registry**, run by Zed Industries, Inc., which serves the
  Extensions page. Their policies govern searching it and installing from it.

The current version of this document lives at
<https://knightcode.dev/legal/acceptable-use>.
