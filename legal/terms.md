whole of the agreement that governs your use of it, and it is the only one.

agree terms about.

Two things the licence does not cover, said plainly here instead:

- **Your AI provider's terms are between you and them.** KnightCode signs you
  in to accounts you already have — Anthropic, OpenAI, GitHub Copilot, or an
  API key you supply — and sends your prompts to whichever of them you choose.
  What they may do with those prompts is set by their terms, not ours. We never
  see them: the credential and the request both stay on your machine.
- **Extensions come from the Zed extension registry**, run by Zed Industries,
  Inc. Opening the Extensions page and installing an extension sends a request
  to `zed.dev` and runs code we did not write. Their terms apply to that.
